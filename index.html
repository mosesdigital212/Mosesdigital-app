2. Import & Export Resources

Practical resources to help individuals and businesses understand international trade and cross-border transactions.

Import Documentation Checklist

  • Commercial invoice
  • Packing list
  • Bill of lading or airway bill
  • Certificate of origin, where applicable
  • Import permits, where required
  • Customs declaration documents
  • Applicable inspection certificates

Documentation requirements depend on the goods and destination.

Export Documentation

  • Export commercial invoice
  • Packing list
  • Certificate of origin, where required
  • Export customs declaration
  • Transport documentation
  • Product-specific certificates and permits

International Shipping Guide

  • Understand Incoterms.
  • Compare sea and air freight.
  • Understand freight charges.
  • Check cargo insurance.
  • Confirm shipping schedules.
  • Plan customs clearance.

Kenya Import & Export Authorities

Find official information from these institutions:

  • KenTrade
